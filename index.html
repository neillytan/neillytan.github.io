I am a sociotechnical scholar and design researcher studying the ethical, social, and political implications of personal data and technology systems, drawing on HCI, critical data studies, and STS. My current research centers on power dynamics and possibilities for resistance around everyday surveillance technologies, such as the smart home camera, particularly in domestic care work.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Human-Centered Design and Engineering at the University of Washington, co-advised by Drs. Sean Munson and Audrey Desjardins. Currently, I am a Martha H. Duggan Fellow in Caring Labor at the UW Harry Bridges Center for Labor Studies, and my work has been supported by a National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship.
